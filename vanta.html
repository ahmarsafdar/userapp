Frames

Six moments from the cut

The hook

It opens on a single drop hitting a puddle — a quiet beat — and then the sneaker lands in a splash. The product is on screen within the first seconds, before anyone thinks about scrolling.

The build

Slow-motion splashes and a shoe floating in the rain show off the sole and the materials, then the pace picks up with a run into a neon-lit tunnel.

The payoff

A foot steps down in front of the red VANTA sign and the shoe comes to rest on wet, glittering asphalt — brand and product together in the final frame, so the viewer knows exactly what to buy.
